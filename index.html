Older people in regional NSW can keep their balance, close to home, in a class run by their own community.

Steady Together is a weekly one-hour balance and strength class. Local volunteers lead it. Physiotherapists design it, train the leaders and stay responsible for safety. It runs in the local hall, and it keeps running.

Three numbers

  • 41,000

    people are admitted to hospital in NSW after a fall each year.

    NSW Clinical Excellence Commission, 2021
  • 4.3 years

    is how long people stay, on average, in the peer-led New Zealand program this model is based on. Some stay ten.

    Age Concern Otago cohort
  • A$12,800

    per quality-adjusted life year for peer-led group exercise, against A$98,300 when the same class is delivered by paid professionals.

    Deverall et al.; UK economic evaluation. Converted, nominal.

Sources for every figure on this site are listed on the evidence base page.

Where do you fit?

  • You run a community organisation

    A hall, a morning a week and a few willing members is most of what it takes. We bring the program, the training and the clinical backing.

    Host a program

  • You are an older person

    Come to a class, or train to lead one. Leaders are ordinary people from the community, not health professionals.

    Join or lead a class

  • You are a clinician

    Physiotherapists, GPs and exercise physiologists govern the program. Roughly 10–15 hours in the first year, then 4–8 hours a year.

    Support the program
