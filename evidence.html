What the evidence does not show

A page that only sells is not evidence. Four honest caveats.

No direct comparison exists
No trial has compared volunteer-led against clinician-led delivery on falls outcomes. Anyone claiming the volunteer model is proven equivalent is going beyond the evidence.
Clinician-led delivery may work better in the short term
A physiotherapist individualises more precisely and spots more. Over ten weeks, we would expect clinician-led to win.
The programs cited differ from each other
And from this proposal, in content, intensity and population. Evidence from one is suggestive for another, not transferable.
The economic figures are indicative
Different countries, health systems and years, converted at one nominal rate. They show the scale of a difference, not an Australian cost estimate.

Why the case still holds

Clinician-led weekly classes are not available in most regional communities and are not going to become available. The real comparison is a volunteer-led class running for years against no program at all — and a program people stay in for 4.3 years has a long time to work in.
