A balance class, run by the community, that does not stop after ten weeks.

One hour a week in the local hall. Trained volunteers lead it. Physiotherapists design it and stay responsible for safety.

Get involved How it works
  • 41,000

    NSW hospital admissions after a fall, every year.

    Clinical Excellence Commission
  • 4.3 years

    Average time people stay in the peer-led New Zealand program this model follows.

    Age Concern Otago
  • A$12,800

    Cost per healthy year gained when peers lead the class. Paid professionals delivering the same class: A$98,300.

    Deverall et al.; UK evaluation
