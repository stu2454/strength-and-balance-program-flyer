A note on the currency conversions

The two cost-per-QALY figures and the return-on-investment range are converted from British pounds at a nominal rate of £1 = A$1.91. The source figures use 2010–2011 price bases. They are presented to show the scale of the difference between delivery models. They are not current-dollar estimates of what this program would cost in Australia today, and should not be quoted as such.
