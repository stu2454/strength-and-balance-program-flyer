Get involved

The program needs three things: somewhere to meet, people to lead, and clinicians to govern.

Host a program

For neighbourhood centres, COTA branches, Men's Sheds, service clubs, ACCHOs, councils and church halls.

You provide

  • A room, ninety minutes a week
  • One or two members to train as leaders
  • Your existing public liability and volunteer insurance
  • An admin contact, about an hour a month

You receive

  • The whole program: exercises, forms, agreements
  • Free training and accreditation for your leaders
  • A named clinician and scheduled review visits
  • Local promotional material and help with enrolments

Only host if you expect to still be running this in three years. A class that starts and stops does more harm than one that never starts.

Become a leader

Leaders are ordinary people from the community, usually older people themselves. No health qualification, no fitness background, no teaching experience needed.

Training
Two days, run locally by a physiotherapist, then a half-day refresher each year. Free, with travel covered.
Weekly commitment
About ninety minutes. Leading in pairs is normal, so you are covered when away.
We ask for
Twelve months to begin with. Most stay much longer.
It suits
People who are reliable, warm with a group, and happy to say “let's ask the physio”.

Support the program as a clinician

For physiotherapists, exercise physiologists and GPs. Your time is the binding constraint, so here is the ask first.

  • 10–15 hours

    Total, across the establishment year.

    Author's estimate
  • 4–8 hours

    Per year after that.

    Author's estimate

Spent on

  • Leader training, mostly in year one
  • Quarterly review visits
  • Occasional calls from leaders
  • Reading one-page incident forms

Not asked of you

  • Running the weekly class
  • Taking participants on as patients
  • Carrying the host's insurance or admin

You will get a written role description for your indemnity insurer before you commit. Whether the role is paid or voluntary depends on how your site is funded, and we will tell you which before you agree to anything.
